Carnatic Vocal

Seven years of training, and a stage I keep going back to.

Since 2018 I’ve trained and performed as an independent Carnatic vocalist with Keerthana School of Arts, performing individually and in groups across events in Hyderabad. In 2024 I was lead vocalist for a music festival led by the Telangana State Government, and I’m a winner of the International Music Premier League award at grade level in my 9th standard.

Nityaragam

Music that never stops

Nitya means every minute, every second, eternal. Ragam means music. Put together — nityaragam — it means something like “music that never stops.” That’s the name of a blog my mother started years before I was born, to keep a record of her own lifelong music journey.

I didn’t grow up being taught music so much as growing up inside it. I have now taken over Nityaragam as an initiative with the objective of making Carnatic music accessible to Gen Z.
