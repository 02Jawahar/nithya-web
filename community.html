Nonprofit Leadership Hyderabad Chapter

STEM For Others

President

STEM For Others is a nonprofit run entirely by high schoolers, built on a simple idea: if you love science and math, the best thing you can do with that is hand it to someone who hasn’t had the chance to love it yet. As President of our Hyderabad chapter, I lead a team that brings afterschool STEM programs and resources directly into our community.

The piece I’m proudest of is one we rebuilt from the ground up: one-on-one homework help and core-concept tutoring in science, redesigned to actually reach the students who need it most rather than the ones who already have support at home.

Financial Literacy Grades 6–8

Finance 4 Future

Financial Literacy Instructor

Finance 4 Future brings financial literacy education to low-income households. I teach Grade 6–8 students how to help manage their family’s finances — not as an abstract subject, but as a skill they can put to use at home right away. Each cohort runs for two months, with regular assignments and tests, and a weekly hour set aside just for group brainstorming and lesson planning so the material actually lands.

Environmental Outreach

Waste Recycling Awareness Campaign

Gandipet, Hyderabad

I visited local waste treatment plants to understand how segregation actually works on the ground, and used what I learned to identify strategies that could help them recycle faster. Alongside that, I designed educational posters to help households in the area understand basic waste awareness — segregation, recycling, and why it matters before the truck ever arrives.
