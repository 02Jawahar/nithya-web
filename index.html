Hello, I’m Nitya Lanka — I work at the intersection of music, science, and service.

I’m a senior at Oakridge International School, Hyderabad. I’ve trained and performed as an independent Carnatic vocalist since 2018, spent two summers researching plastics recycling in an R&D lab, designed a self-deorbiting CubeSat, and lead a nonprofit chapter bringing STEM education into my community.

Nityaragam

“Music that never stops.”

Nitya means every minute, every second, eternal. Ragam means music. Put together, nityaragam is a blog my mother started years before I was born, to keep a record of her own lifelong music journey. I’ve now taken it over as an initiative to make Carnatic music accessible to Gen Z.
